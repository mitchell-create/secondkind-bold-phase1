# TikTok Love Letter (organic)

**Output:** video · **Platforms:** TikTok + IG Reels · **Lift:** low · **Brand-intro:** ~60-70% in

## What this is

A long-form text monologue (150-300 words) overlaid on silent B-roll. The text is the hero; the video is visual rhythm. Calls out the persona directly in the opening line.

## When to use it

- Problem-aware personas with high emotional pain
- Matrix positions where the persona feels betrayed by the category mainstream
- Briefs that already have rich PASTOR copy you can repurpose

## Worked example, Danielle bloating brief

**Brief:** `secondkind-bold-gut-balance-0c7dc7.yaml`
**Persona:** Done-Everything Danielle (problem_aware)
**Pain:** Persistent bloating that shows up even on clean-eating days

### The content

**Opener (first 3 seconds, white text full-screen):**

> *Listen, girls who eat clean all day and still look 3 months pregnant by 7pm.*

**Body monologue (lines reveal one at a time over the next 50-60 seconds):**

> *You did everything right today. Oatmeal for breakfast. Salad at lunch. Skipped the wine.*
> *And by dinner your jeans don't fit.*
> *You're not imagining it. You're not being dramatic.*
> *You're on your third probiotic in two years and your gut is still running its own agenda.*
> *Here's what nobody tells you. Probiotics are live bacteria. They have to survive your stomach acid. Then your small intestine. Then somehow colonize a gut that already has its own crew.*
> *Most of them don't make it.*
> *That's not a flaw in a specific brand. That's the structural reality.*
>
> [BRAND INTRO, line 11 of 14]
> *Gut Balance doesn't send live bacteria. It sends what your gut actually uses, the bioactive compounds bacteria produce, already processed, already active the moment they arrive.*
> *No survival gauntlet. No colonization battle.*
> *Just the output your body was supposed to get the whole time.*

## How to produce the visuals in Higgsfield

The video needs silent lifestyle B-roll under the text, no people speaking, just mood. Generate 4-6 clips, edit them in CapCut behind the text overlay.

**IMPORTANT:** Higgsfield should produce **clean visuals only, no text, no captions baked into the image.** The text monologue is added in CapCut afterwards.

**Paste these into Higgsfield Create Image (or Higgsfield Video for direct video gen), generate vertical 9:16:**

```
1. "Shot on iPhone, candid. Hands stirring oatmeal in a cream stoneware bowl, golden morning light through a kitchen window, slightly imperfect framing, no faces visible (only hands and forearms), 9:16 vertical, shallow depth of field. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."

2. "Shot on iPhone, candid. Colorful salad bowl on a wooden counter, a hand placing a slice of avocado on top, midday natural light, slightly off-center composition, 9:16 vertical. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."

3. "Shot on iPhone, candid. Empty kitchen counter at dusk, single ceramic mug, low warm light from one window, no people, contemplative mood, 9:16 vertical, slightly imperfect framing. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."

4. "Shot on iPhone, candid. Hands closing a laptop on a wooden table, evening light, intimate domestic moment, 9:16 vertical, no face visible. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."

5. "Shot on iPhone, candid. Woman's hands on a denim waistband, trying to button jeans, soft natural light, close-up, no face visible, 9:16 vertical. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."

6. "Shot on iPhone, candid. A small handful of pill capsules in an open palm, kitchen counter background, soft morning light, no face, 9:16 vertical. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."
```

**Tips for Higgsfield:**
- Generate all 4-6 scenes in one session for visual tone consistency (lighting, color palette)
- Avoid faces, the text overlay is the focal point; faces compete with it
- Use "phone snap" or "candid" in every prompt to avoid art-directed look
- Reject any output that looks like a stock photo
- ALWAYS include the no-text clause, AI will hallucinate captions/labels otherwise

### Production workflow

1. Lift the PASTOR copy from the brief
2. Adapt opener into a TikTok-comment-style callout
3. Generate 4-6 B-roll clips in Higgsfield using the prompts above
4. Drop into CapCut, add text overlay with line-by-line reveal
5. Export vertical 9:16

### Pitfalls

- Opener feels generic ("Hey ladies"), must mirror the persona's exact inner monologue
- Brand introduced too early, kills the format. Wait until line 10+
- B-roll has faces or talking, destroys the format. Hands/bodies/setting only.
- Wellness-y sunrise yoga shots, too dramatic, will feel preachy. Keep it quiet, domestic.
